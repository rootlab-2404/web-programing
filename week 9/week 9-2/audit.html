02 · Problems I avoided

Two colour choices looked fine to the eye but fail the numbers, so I did not use them for text:

RejectedRatioUsed instead
Bright orange as text on paper2.20:1 FailDark orange #b83c05 (5.33:1)
Bright teal as text on paper1.65:1 FailDark teal #0b6477 (6.34:1)

Bright orange and teal stay as backgrounds behind dark ink text, where they pass easily.

03 · Built in on every page

  • lang="en" on the page, so screen readers pick the right voice.
  • A "Skip to main content" link that appears on first Tab.
  • One h1 per page and headings that do not skip levels.
  • A thick, high-contrast :focus-visible outline on every control.
  • Real button, label and nav elements, with aria-expanded, aria-checked and live regions where state changes.
  • prefers-reduced-motion turns off transitions and smooth scrolling.
  • Layouts that stack on narrow screens, with no sideways scrolling.
