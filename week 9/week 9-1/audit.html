3

Built into every page

The same checklist applies to all six Week 9 pages.

  • lang="en" so screen readers use the right voice.
  • A "Skip to main content" link, shown on the first Tab.
  • header, nav, main and footer landmarks.
  • One h1 and headings that never skip a level.
  • A 3px amber :focus-visible outline on every control.
  • aria-current="page" marks the current page in the menu.
  • Live regions announce results (ratios, scores, errors).
  • prefers-reduced-motion turns off all animation.
  • Layouts stack on small screens. No sideways scrolling at 320px.
  • Text uses rem sizes, so zooming to 200% works.
