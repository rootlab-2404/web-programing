Keyboard playground

Put your mouse away. Everything on this page works with the keyboard alone, because some people can't use a mouse at all.

TabShiftEnterSpaceEsc
1

A switch

A real button with role="switch" and aria-checked.

Tab to it and press Space or Enter. A screen reader announces "Dark preview, switch, on" or "off".

Preview box. The switch changes this box between light and dark. State lives in aria-checked, so the look and the announcement always match.
2

A disclosure

A button controls a region, and aria-expanded tells assistive tech whether it is open.

Why a real button? It gets focus, Enter and Space handling, and the right role for free. Press Esc to close this panel and return focus to the button.

Button vs. clickable div

<div onclick>: no focus, no keyboard, no role.

What I learned

  • WCAG 2.1.1 Keyboard: every feature must work without a mouse.
  • WCAG 2.4.7 Focus Visible: you must always be able to see where focus is. Here it's a thick amber outline.
  • WCAG 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value: custom controls need the right role and state (role="switch", aria-checked, aria-expanded).
  • Native HTML elements give you most of this for free. A div gives you nothing.
