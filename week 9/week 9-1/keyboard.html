Exercise 1

Keyboard playground

Put your mouse away. Use Tab, Shift+Tab, Space and Enter to operate everything on this page.

1. A switch

Built from a button with role="switch". Space or Enter toggles it, and screen readers announce on/off.

2. A disclosure

A button controls a region. aria-expanded tells assistive tech whether it is open.

Real buttons get focus, Space and Enter handling for free. A clickable <div> gets none of that.

3. Live log

Every key you press and every element that receives focus is written here. Click in the box below, then type.
