What every page here does

  • Starts with a skip link and uses header, nav, main and footer landmarks.
  • Has exactly one h1, and headings that never skip a level.
  • Shows a thick, high-contrast focus outline on every link, button and field.
  • Uses colours that all pass WCAG AA. The numbers are on the self-audit page.
  • Turns off animation for people who set prefers-reduced-motion.
