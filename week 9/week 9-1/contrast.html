"Fix it" moves the text colour towards black or white, whichever works, in small steps until normal text reaches 4.5:1. The hue stays as close as possible.

What I learned

  • WCAG 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum): 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text (24px, or about 19px bold).
  • WCAG 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast: borders, icons and focus rings need 3:1 against what is next to them.
  • The ratio comes from relative luminance: (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05). Bright colours like orange and light green usually fail as text on white.
  • The fix is usually to darken the same hue, not to change the brand colour.
